# Privacy and security checklist

> **This is a technical control inventory and review checklist, not legal advice.** Technical controls do not by themselves make the business compliant with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) as amended, or with regulator guidance. The business and an appropriately qualified Malaysian privacy adviser must review the "Business and legal review" section, confirm what the current law and guidance require (including any amendments in force, registration/notification duties, data-protection-officer and breach-notification requirements, and cross-border transfer rules), and adopt the notices and procedures. Section references to the Act are deliberately not given here.

## 2. Operational procedures (templates — adapt)

### Secret rotation

- **WhatsApp access token**: create a new system-user token, set `WHATSAPP_ACCESS_TOKEN`, redeploy, revoke the old one. Quarterly, and after any staff change among people with Meta access.
- **WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET / VERIFY_TOKEN**: rotate in Meta, set env, redeploy (brief window where deliveries fail signature checks and are retried by Meta).
- **APP_SECRET** (signs manage links/hold tokens): rotating invalidates _all_ outstanding manage links and holds; do it only after suspected compromise and notify customers via WhatsApp that links were reset.
- **CRON_SECRET**: rotate in host settings and the scheduler together.
- **Database password**: rotate in the provider, update `DATABASE_URL`, redeploy.
- **Staff passwords**: managers can reset (forces change at next login and revokes sessions).

### Incident and breach response notes

1. **Detect/contain**: disable affected users (Team → Disable; revokes sessions), rotate relevant secrets, block the entry point.
2. **Assess**: use `audit_logs` (who/what/when/request id), host logs and `webhook_events` to determine which records and people were affected; preserve evidence (export the audit log before any further changes).
3. **Notify**: the business decides, with its privacy adviser, whether regulator and individual notification duties apply under current Malaysian law and within what time; keep the decision record.
4. **Recover**: restore from backup if needed (`backup-restore.md`), re-run the checklist, document lessons.
5. **Contacts**: maintain an internal contact sheet (owner, privacy adviser, Meta support, hosting support) outside this repository.

### Dependency and vulnerability hygiene

- CI runs `npm audit --omit=dev --audit-level=high`. Review Dependabot/`npm outdated` monthly.
- Known advisory at build time: `braces` (dev-only via `eslint-config-next`); runtime audit clean.

## 3. Security review notes (what was examined and found)

Review performed on the finished code. Defects found _and fixed_ during development: wrong-password login returned a 500 (untyped SQL parameters); login/password-change rate-limit counters were rolled back on failure (brute-force limits ineffective for unknown emails/wrong current password); nominees could not see replacement requests because of RLS (fixed with snapshot columns, which also avoids exposing appointments); manager custom-time input state bug. Each has a regression test.

Residual risks (see `assumptions.md` → Known limitations): no MFA; web bookings not phone-verified; CSP uses `'unsafe-inline'` for scripts; rate limiting relies on trustworthy `x-forwarded-for`; the application server holds the system DB role (a server compromise exposes the tenant's data); audit log purge is manual.

## 4. Business and legal review (for the business + privacy adviser)

- [ ] Confirm the business's status and obligations under the PDPA as currently in force (including amendments and any registration/DPO/breach-notification obligations).
- [ ] Review and adopt the privacy notice (English, and Bahasa Malaysia if required); decide where it is displayed and how updates are versioned.
- [ ] Confirm the legal basis/consent wording for WhatsApp service messages versus marketing, and keep evidence of opt-in.
- [ ] Decide retention periods per data category (settings defaults are placeholders).
- [ ] Review cross-border transfers: where the database (Supabase region), hosting (Vercel region) and WhatsApp/Meta process data; add contractual/notice measures if required.
- [ ] Data processing agreements with hosting, database and messaging providers.
- [ ] Define the data-subject request procedure and who verifies identity; confirm response deadlines.
- [ ] Decide whether staff schedules/KPIs processing needs an employee privacy notice and what staff are told about KPI measurement and WhatsApp reminders (the system keeps reminders opt-out-able and own-data only).
- [ ] Confirm whether customers may include operational notes and train staff not to record health information.
- [ ] Decide breach-response responsibilities and contacts.
- [ ] Review what is stored in WhatsApp conversations and the business's own phone/WhatsApp practices outside this system (direct chats with masseuses are not synchronised unless they use the app).
